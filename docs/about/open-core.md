# Open-core

Plasma is **open source** under [EUPL-1.2](https://github.com/plasmash/.github/blob/main/LICENSE), built in the open at [github.com/plasmash](https://github.com/plasmash). It follows an **open-core** model: the platform primitives are open and community-driven; advanced operational tooling is offered as a managed/commercial layer.

## What's open

The primitives you build and run on:

- The layer architecture and component model
- Component types and their lifecycle
- The ontology framework (entities, metrics)
- Integration patterns (ECST, NATS, relays)
- The `plasmactl` toolchain and core deployment

## What's commercial

Operational tooling where complexity warrants dedicated support:

- Advanced platform automation and multi-tenant management
- Enterprise deployment, monitoring, and alerting
- Professional support and SLAs — via **[Plasma Cloud](https://www.skilld.cloud/plasma-cloud)**, fully managed Plasma operated by [Skilld](https://skilld.cloud), the creators of Plasma

## Why this split

Platform primitives benefit from community contribution and trust, while operational complexity needs sustained maintenance. A clear boundary lets the project stay healthy and lets you choose how much you self-operate versus delegate.

These docs cover the **open** platform. Where a capability is part of the managed offering, it's called out as such.
